Cookies test

Expected on the dashboard: 4 cookies in total, all set by the site itself: itv_a (written three times, counts once), itv_b, itv_c and itv_gone (written once, then deleted; the deleting write adds nothing). A write without a name (no-equals-sign) is ignored.

To check de-duplication with headers: run python serve_cookies.py from this folder instead of http.server; it also sets itv_a in the response header, and the total must stay 4, not 5.
