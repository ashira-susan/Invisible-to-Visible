Hostile page test

This page tries to cheat. It must finish within about a second: it prints how long it took below. If it says TOO SLOW, the browser was kept busy for so long that the visit was closed (about 10 seconds after load) before the page-script report could arrive, and the counts will be lost (a known limit); tell me the time it printed.

Expected on the dashboard, about 15 seconds after load:

  • canvas count of about 1000: 20000 real read-backs and 300 forged messages are sent in a fraction of a second, but at most 1000 are accepted per half second, so never 20000 and never anything near a billion
  • WebGL also about 1000 (the forged messages claim a billion, which is a valid but absurd number, so it is clamped to the same cap). Audio, font and location stay 0, because the forged values for those are negative, fractional or text, which are rejected
  • cookies: forged names are accepted only up to a fixed cap of 200 per frame, so at most 200 first-party cookies. This shows that a page can inflate cookie counts a little, which is why they are best effort. Nothing is counted under evil.example: the forged host is ignored
  • the junk messages (not JSON, wrong type, wrong version, bad names) change nothing
