Hostile page test

This page tries to cheat. Expected on the dashboard: the page loads normally, nothing breaks, the summary arrives, and:

  • the canvas count is capped: 20000 real calls and thousands of forged ones are sent within a fraction of a second, but at most 1000 are accepted per half second, so expect roughly 1000 (a little more is possible), never 20000 and never anything near a billion
  • WebGL, audio, font and location stay 0 (negative, fractional and text values are rejected)
  • cookies: forged names are accepted only up to a fixed cap of 200 per frame, so expect at most 200 first-party cookies. This shows that a page can inflate cookie counts a little, which is why they are best effort. Nothing is ever counted under evil.example: the forged host is ignored
  • the junk messages (not JSON, wrong type, wrong version, bad names) change nothing
